# Final figure revision strategy

1. Preserve the preceding submission folder and its reviewed manuscript.
2. Regenerate numeric figures from archived arrays with readable Times New Roman labels and a recorded data check.
3. Redraw the recovered pipeline concisely; preserve training logic and use the manuscript indexing.
4. Integrate at natural size, adjust floats only as needed, keep body prose/results/equations unchanged and at most six A4 pages.
5. Independently inspect figures and every PDF page, verify fonts/references/results, then package and commit the final folder and diff.

No new experiments. No unsupported claims or restoration of previously incorrect descriptions.
