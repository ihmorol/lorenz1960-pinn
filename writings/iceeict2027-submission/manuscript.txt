                          VI. CONCLUSION

   Our 27-window PINN approximates the nearly closed
Lorenz-system orbit on [0, 13.26446] with a five-seed median
combined RMSE of 8.13 × 10−5 against DOP853. The seed-
0 RMSE is 6.97 × 10−5, compared with 2.237 for the saved
full-interval network. Separate window networks give lower
final errors under the compared settings, and warm start
reduces Adam updates while improving the matched-seed
accuracy. This provides an effective training structure for the
studied initial-value problem. Future work will test matched
configurations, other initial states and coefficient sets to assess
how well the approach extends beyond this orbit.
