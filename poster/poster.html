01Problem

Approximate one nearly closed orbit of the Lorenz initial-value problem.

ẋ = −0.1 yz
ẏ = 1.6 xz
ż = −0.75 xy

u(0) = (0.5, 0.75, 1.0)ᵀ
t ∈ [0, 13.26446]

02Sequential causal window training

One separate 4 × 60 tanh network per window
W₀ · W₁ · … · W₂₆27 equal windows · 41,446 unique uniform times · Δt ≈ 0.491
01 / WINDOW k

Set state + weights

For k > 0, use the previous predicted endpoint and copy its trained weights.

ûₖ(t) = u₀,ₖ + (t − tₖ)Nₖ(t; θₖ)

Hard initial state: ûₖ(tₖ) = u₀,ₖ exactly.

02 / PHYSICS

Autodiff residual

Pass 1,536 ordered collocation times through the trial solution.

rₖ(t) = dûₖ/dt − f(ûₖ(t))

Lₖ,ᵢ is the mean of three squared residual components.

03 / CAUSAL ADAM

Train in four stages

wₖ,ᵢ = exp[−ε ∑ⱼ<ᵢ Lₖ,ⱼ]
ℒₖ,ε = meanᵢ(wₖ,ᵢ Lₖ,ᵢ)
ε: 0.01 → 0.1 → 1 → 10

Weights are detached during each Adam gradient step.

Advance at verified post-update min(w) > 0.99 or after 4,000 Adam updates.
04 / REFINE

L-BFGS

Minimize the unweighted residual mean.

At most 1,000 optimizer iterations per window.

↶ k + 1
Save and hand off: save window k; set u₀,ₖ₊₁ = ûₖ(tₖ₊₁) and initialize θₖ₊₁ from θₖ. Repeat through W₂₆.
Values meet at 26 interfaces; derivatives are not constrained to match.
After W₂₆ → evaluateAssemble the piecewise prediction; compare on 13,265 times with DOP853 and measure residuals. Reference values do not enter loss, gradient, or stopping.
