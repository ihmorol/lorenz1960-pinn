                          VI. CONCLUSION

   Each of our 27 window PINN passes each of the tests on
the nearly closed Lorenz-system interval [0, 13.26446]. The
next window was predicted to be at the end. The saved run
had a combined state RMSE of 6.97 × 10-5 in comparison
to DOP853. A network designed separately to full intervals
recorded an RMSE of 2.237. Future research will focus on
capacity-matched configurations and additional initial states
and generalise to problems with specified boundary conditions
at each end.
